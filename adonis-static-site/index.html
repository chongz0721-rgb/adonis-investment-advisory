Our Firm

01 — 06

Built for the decisions that change trajectories.

Adonis is an independent global investment and advisory firm serving a select group of leaders at defining moments.

We were founded in 2018 by Adonis Yang and Tommy Chong after a late-night conversation in Pasadena about a widening gap: capital had become more abundant, yet truly integrated judgment had become rare. Their answer was a different kind of firm — one that could see like an investor, build like an operator and advise with absolute discretion.

The origin of Adonis

Two disciplines.
One impossible standard.

From an original two-person studio, Adonis moved quickly: advising a transformative technology carve-out in its first year, opening New York and Shanghai in 2020, and establishing a connected nine-city platform by 2024.

“Never confuse scale with significance. We built Adonis to be present only where the decision truly matters.”
— Adonis Yang, Founder & CEO
